# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + Tailwind CSS + vanilla JavaScript, with GSAP + ScrollTrigger for motion. Native browser scrolling: Lenis smooth scroll and the custom cursor were removed on 2026-10-27 because the owner found the site laggy; fluidity takes priority over effects.

## Users

Rural producers, business owners, public servants, retirees and consumers in Rio Grande do Sul and São Paulo who face a debt, bank contract, rural credit or asset-seizure problem. They arrive worried, often from a phone, and need to feel within seconds that they found a serious, reachable specialist.

## Product Purpose

The one-page site for Elizandra Girardon Advocacia. Success is a visitor opening a WhatsApp conversation with the office.

## Positioning

A lawyer who spent more than 24 years inside the financial system and now defends producers and consumers against it, specialised in both Banking Law and Agribusiness Law.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Practice areas: rural credit, extension and lengthening of debts, over-indebtedness, contract review, executions, search and seizure, bank fraud, asset protection.
- Bar registrations: OAB/RS 100.183 and OAB/SP 543.297.
- Specialisations: Agribusiness Law, Agricultural Policy, Extension of Rural Debts, Judicial Recovery, Banking Law, Defence of the Debtor in Execution.
- Undecided: WhatsApp number, street address, opening hours, Instagram handle. These stay as marked placeholders.
- Advertising must follow OAB Provimento 205/2021: no promise of results, no superlatives such as "the best", no prices, no client testimonials, no commercial capture tone.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Elizandra Girardon Advocacia. Logo: an "EG" monogram built from thin horizontal and vertical lines, with the name in wide-tracked capitals.
- Body typeface Outfit, as on the current site.
- Palette (owner choice, 2026-10-27, replacing navy and amber-gold): wine and sand. Sections alternate dark (wine) and light (sand), with bordô and dune as accents; the look must stand apart from the navy-and-gold default of law-firm sites. Owner direction: contemporary and bold. The hero uses the red-suit portrait (new, higher-quality photo supplied 2026-10-27) and the closing section the white-suit one, on a bordô band.
- Lady Justice statue is wanted (owner request, 2026-10-25): bronze statue with scales, used as a featured image. Still no gavel stock imagery.
- Hero name set in a heavy condensed sans (Anton) with a metallic finish, per owner reference.

## Evidence on Hand

- Office description and lawyer biography texts supplied by the user (real copy).
- Photos: hero portrait in red suit, waist-up (assets/elizandra-hero-bordo.webp, cut out from foto4-bordo.jpg), white-suit full-length cut-out in the closing section (assets/elizandra-final-branco.webp), profile portrait in white blazer, office facade, logo (assets/). All supplied at low resolution; originals still needed.
- No testimonials, client counts or case counts exist, and none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Seriousness first: the visitor must trust before they act.
2. One action: every path leads to a WhatsApp conversation.
3. Speak the client's problem, not legal jargon.
4. Informative and sober, never salesy, per OAB rules.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard navigation, works without JavaScript. Motion runs even when the system asks for reduced motion: owner decision (2026-10-25), because the owner wants the animation always visible. Known trade-off for motion-sensitive visitors. Audience includes older readers on phones, so body text stays generous.
