# Design System: Elizandra Girardon Advocacia

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Ruled Ledger at Night"**

A deep navy field runs edge to edge and is never broken by a white page. Structure on it is drawn, not built. Thin amber and ivory hairlines divide, underline and track progress. They share their straight-stroke vocabulary with the EG monogram, whose six strokes are redrawn as SVG lines that draw themselves when they appear. Type does the monumental work. Her name stands in tall Anton capitals with a cast-metal finish. Bodoni Moda at very large sizes carries section titles and the client's own phrasing of a problem. Outfit carries everything that must read calmly for an older visitor on a phone.

Density is low and the sections form bands. Each one is a full-width navy band, one step lighter or darker than its neighbour, with generous vertical padding. Amber is the only warm, saturated colour. The lawyer's red blazer in the closing photograph is the only red (since 2026-10-27 the hero uses the white-suit portrait and the closing band the red-blazer one). Photography sits low in the field: the office facade dimmed behind the hero, and cut-outs (the lawyer twice, a bronze Lady Justice once) that feather into the navy. Motion is exponential and editorial: a drawn-monogram preloader, masked word reveals, drawn strokes, words that light as you read and a pinned timeline. On a mouse, the page also answers the hand. An amber ring follows the pointer, actions pull toward it, and figures and photographs turn under it. The page is complete and legible without any of it.

The system rejects the law-firm default of gavels and rows of equal boxed service cards. Content that would otherwise be cards is set as ruled rows or as stages on a timeline. The one classical emblem is a bronze Lady Justice, used once, to introduce the practice areas.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Navy ground in four tonal steps; sections band by tone, not by borders or shadows.
- Amber is the single accent: actions, hairlines, figures, monogram, pointer, the light behind cut-outs.
- Anton capitals for her name, Bodoni Moda at monumental scale for titles, an Outfit body in ivory.
- Hairline rules, never boxes; square corners, with circles reserved for the WhatsApp float, the contact buttons and the pointer layer.
- Drawn, masked, expo-out motion plus a fine-pointer interaction layer, with a full no-JS fallback.

## Colors

A cold, nocturnal navy ramp lit by one warm metal, amber-gold, with ivory for text.

### Primary
- **Harvest Amber** (`amber`): the primary action slab and every accent hairline, at reduced alpha. Drawn rules use 0.55, the contact-button frames 0.45, the timeline spine and the hero credentials rule 0.3, the timeline progress track 0.22, the marquee band edges 0.20 and the scrolled header edge 0.18. At full strength it fills the 2px reading-progress bar, the cursor dot, the labelled cursor disc and the hovered contact buttons. The cursor ring is drawn at 0.7 and warms to 0.12 over links, and the clause sweep runs at 0.09. As light, it forms the halo behind the hero portrait and the glow behind the statue (0.34 at the centre to 0.12 at 55%, clear by 80%). It also covers the monogram, timeline markers, statistic figures, the preloader counter (at 0.7), the focus ring and text selection.
- **Pale Straw** (`amber-soft`): the amber that sits beside text. It is the primary button's hover state and colours the italic hero subtitle, remedy lines in the timeline, practice-area labels, and the hovered clause phrase and FAQ question.

### Neutral
- **Midnight Ledger** (`navy-950`): the deepest band. It grounds the preloader, the marquee band, the ano-safra timeline, the closing call to action, the footer and the scrolled header (at 0.93 alpha with blur), and it is the ink on amber surfaces, including the cursor label and hovered contact buttons.
- **Bank Navy** (`navy-900`): the default page ground and `theme-color`. It carries the hero, office statement, Lady Justice intro, lawyer and FAQ bands. Over the hero photograph it is the shade, at 0.15 to 0.72 alpha, solid at the foot.
- **Vault Navy** (`navy-850`): the address band.
- **Statement Navy** (`navy-800`): the lightest band, reserved for the banking clause list.
- **Paper Ivory** (`ivory`): headings, marquee words, contact icons and primary text. Body copy uses it at reduced alpha: 0.85 lead, 0.80 prose, 0.75 secondary and credentials, 0.70 footer, 0.55 legal line. Hairline dividers use 0.10 to 0.15 and the secondary button frame uses 0.30.
- **Steel Mist** (`ivory-muted`): small labels above data (state names, "Endereço", "Atendimento") and the "ADVOCACIA" line of the wordmark.

Some values sit outside the palette and are never reused as colours:
- **Hero name metal:** the gradient stops (#FFFFFF, #EFE8DB, #A8A296, #D9C29A) belong to that finish only. See Typography.
- **Scrollbar thumb:** a navy step (#2A4675) on a navy-950 track, in browser chrome only.
- **Tilt glare:** a warm light, described under Elevation & Depth.

### Named Rules
**The One Red Rule.** Red exists only in the closing-band photograph. No token, rule, button or illustration is red. (Owner swapped the hero and closing portraits on 2026-10-27.)

**The One Metal Rule.** Amber is the only accent hue. Hierarchy within it comes from alpha and from the amber / amber-soft pair, never from a second accent. The hero name's cast-metal finish is a material, not a second accent, and it appears nowhere else.

**The Banded Ground Rule.** Sections separate by stepping the navy tone (950 / 900 / 850 / 800), not by cards, borders around blocks or shadows.

## Typography

**Name Font:** Anton (with Bebas Neue, Impact, sans-serif), weight 400, uppercase. It is used for the hero name only.
**Display Font:** Bodoni Moda (with Didot, Bodoni 72, Georgia, serif), optical sizing on, weight 400, roman and italic.
**Body Font:** Outfit (with system-ui, sans-serif), weights 400 and 500.

**Character:** her name stands in a condensed grotesque, tall and cast in metal, like letters on a building. Everything that speaks is in a high-contrast Didone set tight at -0.02em. A soft geometric sans reads gently at body size.

### Hierarchy
- **Display name** (Anton 400, uppercase, line-height 0.88, 0.004em): the hero name, broken onto two lines, "ELIZANDRA / GIRARDON", preceded by a small "DRA." at 0.27em, top-aligned before the E, with the same metal fill and the same rise, hop and wave motion. It uses `display-name-sm` below 1024px and `display-name` from 1024px. Its fill is a vertical metallic gradient clipped to the letters: #FFFFFF at 0%, #EFE8DB at 34%, #A8A296 at 68% and #D9C29A at 100%.
- **Display** (400, line-height 1): the two practice-area section titles, "Agronegócio" and "Bancário". Larger statement headings use the same face at clamp(2.4rem, 5.2vw, 5.25rem) beside the statue and at clamp(2.6rem, 6vw, 5.75rem) in the closing band, held to 16 to 18ch. The office statement that lights word by word runs at clamp(2.1rem, 4.4vw, 4.25rem).
- **Headline** (400, line-height 1.02): standard section headings.
- **Marquee** (400 italic, line-height 1.1): the running band of practice areas, in ivory with monogram separators.
- **Title** (400, line-height 1.375): clause rows. FAQ questions sit one step down at clamp(1.3rem, 2vw, 1.7rem), and timeline stage names are 2.4rem at line-height 1.
- **Subtitle** (400 italic, Pale Straw): the hero's practice line, `subtitle` on mobile and `subtitle-lg` from 1024px.
- **Figure** (400, tabular lining numerals, amber): statistics. The preloader counter uses the same treatment at 3rem, amber at 0.7.
- **Body** (400, line-height 1.65): the default. Long prose runs at `body-long`, capped at 44 to 62ch.
- **Caption** (400): secondary single lines in Outfit, such as practice-area labels in clause rows, statistic labels and the hero credential line.
- **Action** (500, 0.025em): primary button labels and the drawn-underline text link. Timeline remedy lines share the size at weight 400.
- **Nav** (400): desktop header links and the hero credentials row (tabular, ivory at 0.75).
- **Label** (400, 0.025em): data labels and registration lines, in Steel Mist or Pale Straw.
- **Wordmark** (500, set in capitals): the logotype beside the monogram only. `wordmark-sub` sets the "ADVOCACIA" line. The cursor's "WhatsApp" / "Mapa" label uses the same 0.7rem size at weight 500 and 0.14em, in capitals.

### Named Rules
**The Cast Name Rule.** The hero name is the only Anton and the only gradient-filled text in the system. The owner chose the finish from a reference image. It is sanctioned for the hero name alone and does not license gradient text anywhere else.

**The Italic Voice Rule.** Bodoni italic marks the client-facing voice: the hero subtitle, the marquee of practice areas and the list of who the office defends, separated by upright amber slashes.

**The Tabular Registration Rule.** OAB numbers, figures and counters always use tabular lining numerals.

## Layout

The page sits in a centred container (max 84rem) on a 12-column grid at desktop. Side gutters are 1.25rem, 2rem from 640px and 3rem from 1024px. Text columns are offset (col-start 3, 8 or 10) rather than centred, so headings and prose sit asymmetrically. Section bands use vertical padding of 6rem, 8rem from 640px, and 10 to 11rem from 1024px; the closing band runs to 12rem. The header is 4.5rem tall, 5rem at desktop. The marquee band is a thin full-bleed strip between the hero and the office statement, with 1.5rem of vertical padding (2rem from 640px).

From 1024px the hero is a full-viewport stage (100svh, min 44rem) layered in depth. At the back is the dimmed facade photograph. Next comes the name, left-aligned in the container 14svh from the top and sitting behind the portrait. The halo and cut-out portrait are centred at 73% across, and the portrait (92svh tall) overlaps the end of the letters. The copy sits bottom-left, at most 30rem wide and 7svh off the floor: subtitle, lead, primary action with a text link, then the credentials row under a 1px amber rule at 0.3. Below 1024px the stage is 70svh (min 30rem). The portrait stands centred and the name sits in front of it, centred at the foot of the stage, with the copy stacked beneath.

The Lady Justice intro opens the practice areas. From 1024px the heading and lead take the left seven columns (min 46rem tall) and the statue fills the band's height at the right edge. Below 1024px the statue follows the text, oversized (115% wide) and pushed off the right edge.

The ano-safra timeline is the signature layout. On mobile and without JS, it is a vertical list with a left amber spine. At 1024px with motion on, it pins during scroll and becomes a horizontal track of stages, each min(26rem, 33vw) wide, with a hairline progress bar that fills as you scroll.

The closing band sets its heading and action at left, with the white-suit cut-out standing on the band's floor at the right (84% of its height, 4% in). On mobile the cut-out is 30rem tall below the action and runs into the bottom edge.

## Elevation & Depth

Surfaces are flat by default. Depth comes from several sources:
- the tonal navy bands
- the dimmed facade behind the hero
- the radial amber light behind the hero portrait and the statue
- gradient masks that feather every cut-out into the field
- faint ruled field lines (amber at 0.07 to 0.09 opacity) in the hero and timeline
- perspective, on a mouse

Only two elements cast shadows, and both are soft, downward drops, never offset.

The hero background is the office facade at low exposure: opacity 0.24, saturate 0.75 and brightness 0.92, with two navy-900 shades over it. A radial vignette runs from 0.15 at the centre to 0.72. A vertical wash goes from 0.65 at the top, clear at 28%, 0.35 at 68% and solid navy-900 at the foot, so the band joins the page.

Cut-out masks are alpha masks, not colours. Their stops set coverage only and never paint: #000 means fully shown, rgba(0,0,0,.35) partly shown, and transparent hidden.
- **Hero portrait:** a bottom fade (transparent to #000 at 22%) intersects a left fade (transparent, then .35 at 12% and #000 at 30%).
- **Statue:** a bottom fade to #000 at 26% intersects a left fade to #000 at 14%. The statue also carries saturate 1.1 and contrast 1.06 to hold its bronze.
- **CTA cut-out:** a bottom fade only, to #000 at 22%.

Perspective is the interaction layer's depth. The profile photo and the facade tilt in 3D toward the pointer, up to 9 degrees on X and 11 on Y at a 1000px perspective. A warm glare follows the pointer across them: a radial gradient from rgba(255,238,210,.28) to transparent at 55%. The hero portrait turns on rotationY (up to 3.5 degrees, perspective 1200px), the halo, name and background drift at their own rates, and the statue turns up to 5 degrees (perspective 1400px). The glare is light, not a colour token.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Slab lift** (`box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -14px rgba(2, 6, 16, .8)`, hover `0 18px 32px -16px rgba(2, 6, 16, .9)`): the primary button, paired with a 2px rise on hover.
- **Float** (`box-shadow: 0 14px 30px -10px rgba(3, 8, 20, .75)`): the floating WhatsApp button.

### Named Rules
**The Drawn Not Built Rule.** Structure is a 1px line, not a container. If a block needs separating, rule it; do not box it.

**The Feathered Cut-out Rule.** Every cut-out figure stands on the field, not in a frame. Its foot fades out through an alpha mask, and a radial amber light, never a box, sits behind it.

## Shapes

Corners are square (0px) everywhere: buttons, images, the menu toggle and rows. Circles are reserved for three families:
- the floating WhatsApp button with its pulse ring
- the 48px contact buttons
- the pointer layer (cursor ring, labelled disc and dot)

Focus rings round to 2px, and the scrollbar thumb, which is browser chrome, rounds to 10px. The recurring geometry is the straight stroke:
- the EG monogram's six horizontal and vertical lines, with square caps, which also serve as the marquee's separators
- 1px hairline rules that draw in from the left, and the 2px reading-progress bar that fills the same way
- the 20px amber dash before each specialisation
- the two-bar menu toggle that crosses into an X
- the FAQ plus-to-minus marker, built from two amber lines
- the timeline markers, squares rotated 45 degrees: hollow at rest, filled amber with an offset outline when active
- the preloader's exit, a straight clip-path wipe that lifts the screen off from the bottom edge

## Components

### Preloader
A navy-950 screen, shown only when JS motion runs. The monogram draws stroke by stroke (0.9s, stagger 0.09) and the wordmark rises out of masks. A Bodoni counter in the bottom-right corner runs from 0 to 100 over 1.3s. The content then lifts away and the screen wipes upward (clip-path inset to 100% from the bottom, 1.1s, expo.inOut), handing straight into the hero entrance.

### Buttons
Tactile but sober: a solid metal slab and a drawn frame.
- **Shape:** square (0px).
- **Primary:** an amber slab with navy-950 ink in the action style, padded 16px by 24px, followed by a thin drawn arrow. Every WhatsApp action uses it.
- **Hover / Focus:** the background moves to Pale Straw, the button rises 2px with a deeper shadow, and the arrow slides 4px right, all over 0.4s on the expo-out curve. Focus shows the global 2px amber outline at a 4px offset.
- **Magnetic:** on a fine pointer, primary buttons, contact buttons and the WhatsApp float lean toward the cursor. They follow 0.32 of the horizontal offset and 0.4 of the vertical (0.6s, power3.out), then spring back on leave (1s, elastic.out(1, 0.4)).
- **Line (secondary):** transparent with a 1px ivory frame at 0.30 alpha and ivory text at 0.9rem, padded 12px by 20px. On hover the frame turns amber and the text Pale Straw.
- **Contact:** a 48px hairline circle (amber at 0.45) holding a 20px ivory line icon for WhatsApp, Instagram, phone or map. On hover and focus it fills amber with navy-950 ink over 0.4s, and the cursor names the destination. Contact buttons appear as a row in the address band and the footer.
- **Text link:** ivory or Pale Straw, with an underline that draws from 0 to 100% width on hover and focus.

### Navigation
The header is fixed and transparent over the hero. Once the page scrolls, it gains navy-950 at 0.985 alpha (no backdrop blur, for scroll performance) and an amber hairline at 0.18, and on desktop it compacts from 80px to 68px. A 2px amber reading-progress bar sits on its bottom edge and fills from the left across the whole page. Past 320px of scroll, the header slides away when you scroll down and returns when you scroll up (0.6s, expo.out). It stays put while the mobile menu is open.

The logo redraws its six monogram strokes on hover, and the wordmark opens its tracking to 0.34em in Pale Straw.

Desktop links (Escritório, Atuação, A Advogada, Dúvidas, Contato) sit inside a framed bar: 1px amber at 0.22, navy-950 at 0.45, with 10px amber corner brackets at top-left and bottom-right. A sliding indicator (amber at 0.12 with a 1px amber base line) glides to the hovered link (0.6s, expo.out) and rests on the section in view (scroll spy at 45% of the viewport). Each label rolls up on hover to reveal a Pale Straw copy of itself. The WhatsApp line button fills with amber from the left on hover, turning its text and icon navy-950, and is magnetic.

Below 1024px, a square hairline toggle opens a full-height navy-950 sheet with a faint oversized monogram. The sheet lists the five sections in Bodoni at the headline size (2.4rem on phones) with amber arrows, ruled at ivory 0.10. The rule draws in amber and the label shifts 8px in Pale Straw on hover and for the active section. The primary button, the contact buttons and the address with hours sit at the foot. Links rise in a stagger on open.

### Custom Cursor (fine pointers only)
The native cursor is hidden. In its place, a 40px ring (1px amber at 0.7) trails the pointer (0.5s) and a 6px amber dot tracks it almost exactly. The ring keeps its size over links and buttons and never shows a label (owner request). Pressing thickens the ring to 3px. Touch devices never see it.

### Hero (signature)
The stage layers back to front: dimmed facade, field lines, metallic name, amber halo, cut-out portrait, copy. On entrance the facade settles from 1.12 scale over 3s, the name's letters rise out of masks, the portrait lifts into place and the copy fades up. On scroll, the facade drifts down 12%, the portrait sinks, the name rises and the halo swells. On a mouse, each layer drifts at its own rate:
- **portrait:** slides up to 11px and turns up to 3.5 degrees on rotationY (1.2s)
- **name:** up to 17px (1.4s)
- **halo:** up to 45px by 30px (1.8s)
- **facade:** up to 13px (2s)

Hovering a letter of the name makes it hop 14%. The credentials row (OAB/RS, OAB/SP, 24 years in the financial system) sits under a 1px amber rule at 0.3, in tabular nav type.

### Lady Justice
A bronze Lady Justice, blindfolded and raising the scales, cut out from an Unsplash photograph. She stands at the right of the practice-areas intro on navy-900, with an amber glow behind her and her foot and left edge feathered into the field. She rises in on entrance (2s, expo.out) as the glow blooms (2.6s). On scroll she lifts 60px and leans -1.5 degrees. On a fine pointer she turns up to 5 degrees on rotationY and shifts up to 15px against the mouse (1.4s). She is used once; the copy beside her reads her two pans as the bank and the client.

### Marquee
A full-bleed navy-950 strip ruled top and bottom in amber at 0.20. Practice areas run across it in Bodoni italic, separated by 1.5rem amber monograms. One loop takes 38s at rest. Scroll velocity speeds it up to seven times, and the scroll direction sets which way it runs. It eases back to rest speed over 1.4s. Screen readers get the list as a single sentence.

### Clause Rows (signature)
The banking problems are a ruled list of WhatsApp links. Each row sets the client's phrasing in the title style across six columns, the practice area in Pale Straw caption type across five, and a thin arrow in the last column. Rows divide on ivory hairlines at 0.15. On hover or focus, an amber wash at 0.09 sweeps in from the left (0.7s). The phrase indents 12px and turns Pale Straw, the arrow slides 6px and turns amber, and an amber rule draws across the bottom.

### Ano-safra Timeline (signature)
Six stages run from contract to asset risk. Each has a Bodoni stage name, an ivory description, and a Pale Straw remedy line set off by an ivory 0.10 hairline. Markers are rotated hollow amber squares. In the horizontal desktop variant, inactive stages dim to 0.42 opacity, stage names slide in as they enter, and the active stage fills its marker.

### Scroll-lit Statement
The office statement is split into words that start at 0.16 opacity and light to full ivory one by one, scrubbed directly to scroll as the heading passes through the viewport. Without JS it is a full-strength heading.

### Tilt Images
The profile photo and the facade are square frames revealed by a clip-path wipe. On a fine pointer they tilt in 3D under the warm glare described in Elevation & Depth, and return flat on leave.

### FAQ

### Statistics

### Named Rules
**The Pointer Layer Rule.** The cursor, magnetism, tilt, glare, figure follow and letter hops exist only on a fine, hovering pointer. Touch users get the same page with scroll-driven motion alone, and nothing they need is hidden behind a hover.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** separate sections by stepping the navy ground (950, 900, 850, 800) and separate content with 1px hairlines.
- **Do** keep amber as the only accent and vary its strength by alpha; put Pale Straw (`amber-soft`) beside text.
- **Do** set the hero name in Anton capitals with its metallic finish. Set titles and the client's own words in Bodoni Moda at weight 400 with negative tracking. Set running prose in Outfit at 1.06rem or larger. Single secondary lines step down to 0.92 to 0.98rem, and only the wordmark capitals and the cursor label go to 0.7rem.
- **Do** stand cut-out figures on the field, with a feathered alpha-mask foot and a radial amber light behind them.
- **Do** keep photographs behind type at low exposure (the hero facade at 0.24 opacity under navy shades).
- **Do** route every action to WhatsApp through the amber slab, the line button, a clause row, a contact button or a drawn-underline link.
- **Do** animate with the expo-out curve `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`: masked word reveals, rules drawn from the left, monogram strokes drawn in sequence. Use elastic.out(1, 0.4) only for the magnetic return.
- **Do** ship the final, fully visible state in the markup. Hidden start states apply only under the `motion-ready` class, which is removed if GSAP and ScrollTrigger have not started within 3s. At the owner's request, motion runs whether or not the visitor's system asks for reduced motion.
- **Do** gate every pointer effect behind `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- **Do** set OAB registrations, figures and counters with tabular lining numerals.

### Don't:
- **Don't** introduce red anywhere outside the hero photograph.
- **Don't** use gavel imagery, and don't repeat the Lady Justice statue; she appears once, in the practice-areas intro.
- **Don't** set services as a row of equal boxed cards; use ruled rows or the timeline.
- **Don't** clip gradients to text anywhere except the hero name.
- **Don't** round corners on content. Circles belong only to the WhatsApp float and its pulse ring, the contact buttons and the pointer layer. The 2px focus ring and the 10px scrollbar thumb are the only other exceptions.
- **Don't** add hard, offset or coloured shadows; the two soft drop shadows are the whole vocabulary.
- **Don't** add a second accent hue or a light page ground.
- **Don't** treat mask stops, the hero name's metal stops or the tilt glare as palette colours.
